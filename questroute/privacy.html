QuestRoute – Privacy Policy

Developer: 2BKalavun

Last updated: 10 October 2026

QuestRoute is a GPS navigation app for Android and Android Auto with map styles inspired by games. This policy explains what data the app uses and why. In short: no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we run no server that receives your data.

Location

The app uses your precise or approximate location to show your position, calculate routes from where you are and give turn-by-turn guidance. Location permission is optional: you can browse the map and search without it.

While a navigation session is active (started on the phone or in Android Auto), location keeps updating with the screen off or the app in the background so voice guidance continues. An ongoing notification is shown during this time, and the session ends when you arrive, tap End or close the app. Outside navigation the app does not use location in the background.

Your last known position is saved on your device so the map opens where you left it.

Maps, search and routes

To draw the map, find places and calculate routes, the app sends the minimum necessary requests to the services below. These requests carry no account or identity. Like any web request, they reveal your device's IP address to that service.

Data that stays on your device

Settings, saved places, alert points you mark (for example a pothole or speed bump), your last parking spot and recent searches are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded or shared. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Files you import (Google Takeout, KML, GPX) are read on your device. Text or places you share into the app are also read on your device.

The speed camera list (OpenStreetMap data) is built into the app; checking for cameras ahead happens on your device and sends nothing.

Android Auto shows guidance on your car's screen using the same data described here; nothing extra is sent to us.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. The app only learns whether a product was purchased; we never see your payment details.

Feedback and error reports

Nothing is sent automatically. If you choose Send feedback or Share error report, the app prepares a message with your text, the app version, device model, Android version, selected map style and, for error reports, the app's local error log. Your location is not included. You choose the app to send it with (for example email) and decide whether to send it. We use what we receive only to fix problems and reply to you, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

Permissions

  • Location (precise/approximate): show your position and navigate. Optional.
  • Foreground service (location) and notifications: keep guidance running during navigation and show the next instruction.
  • Internet and network state: download maps, search and routes.
  • Vibration: subtle haptic feedback.
  • Google Play Billing: in-app purchases.

Children

QuestRoute is made for drivers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Your choices

You can turn off the location permission at any time in Android settings. You can delete all data the app keeps by clearing its storage or uninstalling it. Since we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing else to delete; if you sent us feedback by email and want it deleted, write to us.
