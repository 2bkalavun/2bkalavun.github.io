Hive Escape – Privacy Policy

Developer: 2BKalavun

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Hive Escape is a puzzle game by 2BKalavun. We do not ask for your name, email or any account information.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares with Google: your device's advertising ID, your IP address (used to estimate your general location), how you interact with ads and the app, and diagnostic information such as app performance. This data is encrypted in transit. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy settings on the game's main menu.

More: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Game statistics and crash reports

To find out which levels are too hard and to fix bugs, the game uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. They collect how you play (for example levels started and finished, stars, hints used, time spent), crash and error reports, and technical details such as device model, Android version and an app-instance identifier. Firebase Analytics in this game does not collect your advertising ID and is not used for advertising. The data is encrypted in transit and processed by Google; analytics data is kept for up to 14 months, crash reports for up to 90 days.

More: Privacy and security in Firebase.

Data we keep

Your game progress (levels, stars, settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it. We have no server of our own and no user accounts.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.
